### [error_handling] `.claude/hooks/pre-tool-use-guard.sh:8` — `F-11`

### [security] `.claude/hooks/pre-tool-use-guard.sh:86-91` — `F-12`

**建议**：替换 `$p` 为 `${p//$'\n'/\\n}`。
