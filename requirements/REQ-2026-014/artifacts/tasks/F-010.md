## 需求描述

改 context/team/engineering-spec/meta-schema.yaml：在 fields: 段追加 worktree 一组定义。enabled (bool, optional)；owner (enum {workflow, external, none}, optional)；path (string, optional)；branch (string, optional)；baseline.status (enum {passed, failed, skipped}, optional)；cleanup.policy (enum {owned-only, never}, optional)。conditional_required 不强制 worktree 段必填（旧需求兼容，缺则默认 enabled=false）。新增 tests/lib/test_meta_schema_worktree.py 验证：含 worktree 段的 meta.yaml 校验通过；worktree.owner 取非法枚举值时校验失败；缺 worktree 段的 legacy meta.yaml 校验通过。落地 outline-design §OD-3 决议 + spec §5.2 + tech-feasibility §1.5 R7 缓解。
