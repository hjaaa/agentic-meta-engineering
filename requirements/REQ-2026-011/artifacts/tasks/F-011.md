## 需求描述

scripts/lib/workflow_continue.py 在 _main_loop 进入前新增 _poll_sub_workflows(run_state, workflow, run_dir, jsonl_path)：扫 run_dir/sub_runs/*/run-state.jsonl 末位事件，子 workflow_completed → 父写 child_graceful_exited + node_completed；子 workflow_failed → 按父 yaml on_subworkflow_failure ∈ {skip|abort|continue} 写 child_failed + node_skipped / node_failed + workflow_failed / node_completed；子 workflow_cancelled → child_force_killed + node_failed。父反扫幂等（多次 continue 不重复写）。落地 AC-08。
